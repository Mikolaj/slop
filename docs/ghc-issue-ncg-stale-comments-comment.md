Posted on 2026-11-16 as a comment on GHC [#28127](https://gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/ghc/-/work_items/28127), under a preface naming Claude as its author; the text from "Two more items" down is the body. It joins two drafts, the users' guide's `-fcmm-sink` sentence, drafted in this file on 2026-11-15, and the false citation in `GHC.CmmToAsm.X86.Ppr`, which GHC [!16995](https://gitlab.haskell.org/ghc/ghc/-/merge_requests/16995) corrects, and a paragraph on the weights added on 2026-11-16 in the verification of that merge request. The quotations were checked against the users' guide at d22e6110be and its parent and against `compiler/GHC/CmmToAsm/X86/Ppr.hs` at master 37ad471050 and 13408a2b59, the paragraph on the weights against the code at 13408a2b59.

Two more items for the list in the description, found while revising the merge request and in the review of !16891:

- Users' guide, `-fcmm-sink`: says that the sinking pass "attempts to find identical Cmm blocks and eliminate the duplicates attempts to move variable bindings closer to their usage sites"; the words before the second "attempts" are those of the `-fcmm-elim-common-blocks` entry, run into this sentence. The sinking pass (Note [Sinking and inlining] in `GHC.Cmm.Sink`) moves assignments closer to their uses.
- `GHC.CmmToAsm.X86.Ppr`, `pprNatCmmDecl`: cites Note [Internal proc labels] in `GHC.Cmm.Label`, across two lines; the Note is in `GHC.Cmm.CLabel`.

!16995 corrects both too: the users' guide's sentence now says only that the pass attempts to move variable bindings closer to their usage sites, and the citation names `GHC.Cmm.CLabel` and is on one line.

With the first item, the users' guide part of the list is two entries, the paragraph of `-fcmm-static-pred` and the sentence of `-fcmm-sink`, where the title and the location line say one paragraph.

The items on `-fcmm-static-pred` and on `mkCmmIfThenElse'` name only the block layout as a use, on x86-64, of the prediction and of the weights that `getCfg` gives the likely annotations. `invertCondBranches` in `GHC.CmmToAsm.X86.CodeGen` uses them too: where a block ends in a conditional jump to the block placed next and a jump to another block, and the next block has an info table, it swaps the two jumps' targets and inverts the condition only if the CFG weighs the edge to the other block more. Neither use reaches i386, which shares x86-64's `NcgImpl` (`ncgX86 = ncgX86_64`): `ncgEnableDeadCodeElimination` (`initNCGConfig` in `GHC.Driver.Config.CmmToAsm`) requires `backendMaintainsCfg`, which holds for x86-64 alone, and without it `cmmNativeGen` keeps no CFG past instruction selection. The users' guide's paragraph and the comment in `mkCmmIfThenElse'`, as !16995 corrects them, name both uses.
